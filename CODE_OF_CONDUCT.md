# Code of Conduct

Olivia Charts welcomes people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Help others participate by keeping discussions respectful, specific, and focused on the work.

- Be considerate when asking questions, reviewing contributions, and disagreeing.
- Critique ideas and code rather than people. Explain the problem and suggest a constructive next step.
- Respect privacy. Do not share someone else's personal information without permission.
- Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexual harassment, and repeated personal attacks are not acceptable.

These expectations apply to the project's issues, pull requests, and other community spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation. Responses should reflect the behavior and its impact, and participants should have a reasonable opportunity to correct misunderstandings.

For abuse, use GitHub's **Report content** or **Report abuse** option on the relevant content or profile. Do not repost harmful material or private details in a public issue. For ordinary project disagreements, explain the technical issue calmly in the existing discussion and ask a maintainer to review it.

## 中文说明

请尊重不同背景与经验的参与者，讨论代码和方案，避免人身攻击。禁止骚扰、威胁、歧视和未经许可公开他人隐私。维护者可移除不当内容、关闭扰乱讨论的帖子或限制参与。遇到滥用行为，请使用 GitHub 的内容或用户举报入口；一般技术分歧可在原讨论中请维护者协助判断。
